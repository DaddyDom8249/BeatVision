# BeatVision Product Guard

**Source:** distilled from the Beatvision-grok product guard and adapted to the original BeatVision application.

## Identity

BeatVision is a song-first AI music director.

> Every Song Has a World. BeatVision Reveals It.

The song is the creative and timeline source of truth. BeatVision is not a generic AI app builder, social feed, prompt playground, or arbitrary project-management product.

## Protect the canonical flow

Song intake → Reveal World → Visual World Report → creator approval → Style Bible → Characters → Environments → Visual Rules → References → Storyboard → Scenes → Motion → Final Video.

## Change gate

A new feature belongs in BeatVision only when it materially improves at least one of:

1. understanding the song
2. revealing its visual world
3. maintaining visual continuity
4. storyboarding the song
5. generating scene visuals
6. animating scenes
7. assembling the final music video

Do not add unrelated functionality merely because it is technically easy.

## Non-negotiables

- Preserve creator control and approval gates.
- Preserve the song as master timeline.
- Preserve world context across every downstream stage.
- Preserve real asset provenance.
- Preserve provider abstraction and honest unavailable states.
- Preserve credit-safe behavior.
- Prefer incremental changes over replacement architecture.
